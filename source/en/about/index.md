# About Us

[NextPilot](https://github.com/nextpilot) is an open platform for intelligent unmanned systems, bringing together enthusiasts in UAVs and robotics from around the world. We focus on overall aerodynamic design, integrated navigation systems, flight control systems, onboard intelligent applications and model-based design. We are committed to delivering an unmannedsystem solution and product family that is easy to use, efficient, reliable and open, and today it is widely used in universities, research institutes and industrial organizations.
