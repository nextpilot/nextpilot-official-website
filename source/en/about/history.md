# History

- 2024.3.21 — After nearly 10 quiet years the team came back to life, publishing the [NextPilot](https://github.com/nextpilot/nextpilot-flight-control) flight control source code on GitHub and Gitee, built on PX4 and RT-Thread.

- 2014 ~ 2024 — Ten years of churn and change; some of our teammates have grown into ministers, CTOs, deputy chief engineers and chief designers, yet our passion for `flight control systems` has never faded.

- 2014.11.1 — A group of flight control engineers at an institute founded the NextPilot flight control project team. Back then it was just an interest group studying the PX4 open-source autopilot.
