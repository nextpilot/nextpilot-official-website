# Status Monitoring

After installation is complete, place the autopilot in an open, unobstructed environment. Once satellite positioning is acquired, you can view the basic status in the ground station.

## Viewing the INS

Click the INS icon in the status bar to show INS status data, including the currently selected INS, operating status, temperature and more.

<img src="/assets/images/manual/status-ins.png" alt="status-ins" loading="lazy" />

## Viewing Position and Attitude

Click the UAV icon on the map to show the flight tag, including UAV position and attitude data, as shown below:

<img src="/assets/images/manual/status-position.png" alt="status-position" loading="lazy" />

You can also click the GNSS icon to show GNSS status data, including the current guidance status (required for moving platform flight), UAV latitude and longitude, heading, fix type, satellite count and more.

<img src="/assets/images/manual/status-gnss.png" alt="status-gnss" loading="lazy" />

## Viewing Airspeed

Airspeed can be viewed in the flight tag or on the instrument panel.

<img src="/assets/images/manual/status-airspeed.png" alt="status-airspeed" loading="lazy" />

## Viewing Voltage

Click the battery icon in the status bar to view traction battery status data, including voltage, remaining capacity and more.

## Viewing Messages

In the General section of the left sidebar, click `Log Analysis` to enter the data monitoring screen, where you can view all data messages sent by the autopilot, including message name and frequency.

<img src="/assets/images/manual/status-message.png" alt="status-message" loading="lazy" />
