# NextPilot Community Support

Welcome to the NextPilot developer community! To foster an open and welcoming development environment, we are committed to strictly following the [NextPilot Code of Conduct](./code-of-conduct.md).

- [Technical Support](./support.md) — get help, troubleshoot issues and scan the QR code to join the group
- [Contributing Code](./contribute.md) — including [code style](https://github.com/nextpilot/nextpilot-flight-control/blob/main/.clang-format) and [commit conventions](./contribute.md#commit-conventions)
- [Improving Documentation](./contribute.md) — help improve our documentation, such as user manuals and development guides
