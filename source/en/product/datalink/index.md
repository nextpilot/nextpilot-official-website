# Data Link

In accordance with the [GB46750-2025 Civil Unmanned Aircraft System Remote Identification Specification](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=56E7666BF313B2F51A747F2BEEE4F896), we provide UAV remote identification signal transmitter and receiver modules to meet compliant flight requirements.
