## Product Details

### Overview

UAV development inevitably involves a great deal of flight testing, which calls for a handy airframe platform: easy to assemble, convenient to store, with plenty of mounting space, complete spare parts and easy to disassemble and swap. We have bought many airframes and were never quite satisfied. Although structural design is not our profession, we wanted to build a UAV airframe for developers with our own hands!

The `NP-SNAIL-550` airframe is a quadrotor "X" configuration. The main body uses a stacked carbon plate structure with vias left in the plates for convenient cable routing. The spatial layout is well designed and accommodates the vast majority of flight controllers. The arms fold inward for easy storage. It is compatible with the mounting dimensions of mainstream propulsion systems (motors and ESCs), satisfying any combination requirement.

All spare parts are standard, mature, off-the-shelf products with reliable and stable supply, which greatly simplifies repair and avoids the high cost of replacing the whole airframe over minor damage. The payload mounting plate supports stacked expansion, doubling the mounting space!

### Basic Capabilities

- 584 mm diagonal wheelbase, 402 mm left-right wheelbase, 422 mm front-rear wheelbase, supporting propellers up to 15 inch.
- Airframe platform weight approximately 500 g.
